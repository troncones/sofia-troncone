It started with my own paycheck.

I get $275 every two weeks from a part-time job. For a long time it was gone in two days.

I wasn't missing the tools. I had a high-yield savings account, and I'd just opened an investing app. I was missing a moment: money only moved when I remembered, and I was never sure how much was safe to move.

So I'm building the thing I needed. It shows up on payday, does the math, and moves the money before I can spend it.
